# KnowUrSchist 🧫

**Overview**: KnowUrSchist is a free UIL (University Interscholastic League, Texas competitive organization) Science practice platform built to make targeted preparation available for Texas students. Given that UIL Science spans six AP-level subjects and an even wider range of topics outside the school curriculum, effective practice is incredibly difficult as you essentially have to work through hundreds of past tests. KnowUrSchist serves as a platform to organize real past questions into a searchable bank, allowing students to practice by subject, unit, and topic rather than repeatedly taking full tests. With it, I was even able to go from answering a few questions on biology out of 20 to a perfect score in just three months!

**Features**:
 - **Targeted Practice** - Practice real UIL Science questions filtered by subject, unit, specific topics (i.e., mitosis, freezing depression), and even formulas (for physics)
 - **Structured Question Bank** - Past questions are organized into JSON objects with question data, answer choices, etc. using Google's Gemini LLM
 - **Active Recall** - Biology students can create fill-in-the-blank study sets using Markdown, alongside pre-made sets
 - **Flashcards** - Dedicated flashcards for reviewing chemistry and physics formulas are available, as well as those for biology vocabulary terms
 - **Practice Analytics** - Analyze most common question topics and keywords on UIL Science tests to guide future practice

**Tech Stack**: HTML, jQuery, Tailwind CSS, GitHub Pages
