# Spelling Tree 🌳

**Overview**: SpellingTree is a completely free competitive spelling and vocabulary training platform built for UIL (University Interscholastic League, Texas competitive organization) competitive spellers and high school students. I created it after starting my school's first competitive spelling team, and with it, we went on to win our school's first-ever state championship in spelling. Because of its ease of use and accessibility, around 200 students across Texas now use the site for competitive spelling and vocabulary preparation.

**Features**:
 - **Classrooms** - Teachers can create classrooms, add students, and share spelling lists
 - **Practice Modes** - Multiple ways to study spelling lists, including traditional practice, flashcards, memory matching, word matching, and multiple-choice vocabulary questions
 - **Multiplayer** - Real-time Kahoot-style multiplayer spelling practice allows users to compete against each other
 - **Community Lists** - Users can create, publish, and discover spelling lists
 - **Competition** - Each list has its own leaderboard, and users can level up while earning badges
 
**Tech Stack**: HTML, jQuery, Tailwind, Express.js, Socket.IO, Passport.js, MongoDB, Heroku
