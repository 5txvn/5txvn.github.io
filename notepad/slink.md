# Slink 🔗

**Overview**: Slink is an alumni networking platform built for students at my former high school. The inspiration for the site after realizing that much of my success during the college application process came from my connections with upperclassmen at colleges I was interested in. Slink is a manifestation of this realization, as it turns an informal network of students into an accessible resources for high schoolers to learn about college, the application process, future careers, and beyond!

**Features**:
 - **Alumni Directory** - Students can browse and filter alumni by their college, major, career, and high school graduation year
 - **Alumni Profiles** - Alumni can share details such as their college/career and can add personal biographies
 - **College & Career Guidance** - Students can reach out to alumni with relevant experiences, either via connecting with alumni in Slink or using external social platforms
 - **Community Forum** - Students and alumnni can create, reply to, and tag posts about college, careers, and other topics

**Tech Stack**: HTML, jQuery, Tailwind, Express.js, Passport.js, MongoDB, Heroku
